# REP-103

REP-103 is ROS Enhancement Proposal 103, "Standard Units of Measure and Coordinate Conventions," written in October 2010. A REP is the way [[ROS 2|ROS]] records a decision so packages do not invent their own axes. REP-103 says distances are metres, angles are radians, the world is ENU, and a body frame is FLU.

## The problem

One driver publishes degrees and z-down. Another publishes radians and z-up. Every subscriber then needs a private conversion, and half of them are wrong. A written convention makes the default boring.

## In this playground

The sensor messages and the rotor command follow REP-103. Gazebo's world is z-up as well. If a sample ever carries a z-down number, the publisher is wrong, not the subscriber. [[PX4 Autopilot]] does not follow this convention. That mismatch only matters if you later talk to PX4.
